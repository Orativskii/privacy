Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, virtual balances, purchased upgrade status, daily rewards, sound and language preferences, and related timestamps on your device. This information is used to save your game and provide its features. The game does not require an account, and the developer does not operate a server that receives your saved game progress.

Rewarded advertising and consent

The game offers optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The partners and processing can depend on your region and privacy choices. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents a consent form before ads are requested. You can revisit your choice through Settings → Ad privacy when that option is available.

For details about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. You can continue playing without watching rewarded ads.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. The game receives purchase status and product information needed to grant or restore purchases; it does not receive your full payment card details. Google processes payment information under its own privacy policy.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing local data does not erase Google's purchase records or data handled by Google or advertising partners; their policies and controls apply to that information. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit, according to Google. For questions about this game's data handling, contact soratik35@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
